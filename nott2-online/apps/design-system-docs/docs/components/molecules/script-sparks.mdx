# ScriptSparks

The table's read-only 9-word improvisation drawer. *"They should never be the answer, only the starting point for the answer."* Pre-written full sentences fight whatever scene the table has already imagined. Instead, `ScriptSparks` folds open to reveal **9 evocative words** (mixing abstract concepts with visceral nouns and verbs like `"Meat"`, `"Wound"`, `"Electronics"`, `"Clanking"`, `"Leverage"`, `"Paranoia"`) — plus optional Act-specific **"Focus the Camera"** questions for the Camera Crew.

<details open>
<summary>Code example</summary>

```tsx
import { ScriptSparks } from '@nott2/design-system';
import { getCombinedFragmentPool } from '@nott2/game-engine';

const fragmentPool = getCombinedFragmentPool({
  suit: 'Clubs',
  act: 1,
  rank: 1,
  playsetId: 'default',
});

{/* Active Player Drawer: 9 Scene Words */}
<ScriptSparks
  words={fragmentPool.activePlayerWords}
  triggerLabel="Need a Spark? — 9 Scene Words"
  trayHint="9 evocative words to spark your narration (grab one or two):"
/>

{/* Camera Crew Drawer: 9 Sensory Words + Focus the Camera Questions */}
<ScriptSparks
  words={fragmentPool.cameraCrewWords}
  questions={fragmentPool.cameraQuestions}
  triggerLabel="Need a Spark? — 9 Sensory Words & Camera Questions"
  trayHint="9 sensory & atmospheric words (grab one or two):"
  questionsLabel="Focus the Camera (ask the Active Player):"
/>
```
</details>

## Why to Use

When players are improvising a scene together, presenting full pre-written sentences (*"Testing the landline..."*, *"I inherited a mysterious key..."*) pitches contradictory realities and forces the player out of their own imagination.

`ScriptSparks` solves this with three rules-faithful principles:

1. **Single evocative words adapt to any location:** Whether the table is in a flooded basement, a car, a cabin, or a hospital hallway, single words like `"Electronics"`, `"Clanking"`, `"Wound"`, `"Dripping"`, or `"Copper"` spark an immediate sensory detail without contradicting the room the table just established.
2. **Two focused drawers inside The Scene:**
   - **Active Player Drawer:** Deals **9 evocative scene words** (`activePlayerWords`) drawn via uniform Fisher-Yates shuffle (zero duplicates) from a multi-axis pool combining **Universal/Playset**, **Suit**, **Act**, and **Rank Group**.
   - **Camera Crew Drawer:** Deals **9 sensory/atmospheric words** (`cameraCrewWords`) alongside the Act's official **"Focus the Camera"** questions from `NotT_2.md` (*"What does it look like?"*, *"What does it smell like?"*, *"Where is the blood coming from?"*, *"Why is there no hope?"*).
3. **Pure read-only inspiration:** Nothing inside `ScriptSparks` is a selectable button or saved to `SceneState`, and clicking **Shuffle Words** deals 9 fresh words excluding the 9 just shown.

## When to Use

> **Do**
> - Place `ScriptSparks` inside the **Active Player** and **Camera Crew** sections of **The Scene** (`ConversationStakesScreen`) and inside **Narrate the Outcome** (`FalloutScreen`).
> - Pass `activePlayerWords`, `cameraCrewWords`, and `cameraQuestions` from `getCombinedFragmentPool()` or `getOutcomeWordPool()` in `@nott2/game-engine`.

> **Don't**
> - Do not put full concrete sentences into `ScriptSparks` — they dictate the scene instead of sparking it.
> - Do not clutter **Escalation** or **The Stakes** with extra `ScriptSparks` drawers; once the scene is established, the table already knows what is at stake.

## Anatomy

```
State 1: Collapsed (!isOpen)
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [lightbulb] Need a Spark? — 9 Scene Words│  <button class={triggerButton} aria-expanded="false">
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘

State 2: Revealed Drawer (isOpen)
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 9 EVOCATIVE WORDS TO SPARK YOUR NARRATION...  [refresh] Shuffle Words  [expand_less] Close │  trayHeader
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [MEAT]  [WOUND]  [ELECTRONICS]  [CLANKING]  [LEVERAGE]                           │  wordsGrid (wordChip × 9)
│ [PARANOIA]  [ALIBI]  [SPLINTER]  [BARRICADE]                                     │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ FOCUS THE CAMERA (ASK THE ACTIVE PLAYER):                                        │  questionsSection (optional)
│ [“What does it look like?”]  [“What does it smell like?”]  [“Is it pitch black?”]│  questionChip × N
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Root (`styles.root`)** — Flex column container (`gap: vars.space.sm`). Returns `null` when both `words` and `questions` are empty.
2. **Trigger Button (`styles.triggerButton`)** — Shown when `!isOpen`. Displays the `lightbulb` icon and `triggerLabel`.
3. **Tray (`styles.tray`)** — Shown when `isOpen` is `true` (`role="region"`, `aria-label={triggerLabel}`).
4. **Tray Header (`styles.trayHeader`)** — Pairs `trayLabel` with `trayActions` (`reloadButton` + `closeButton`).
5. **Words Grid (`styles.wordsGrid`)** — Flex wrap container (`data-testid="spark-words"`) rendering 9 `wordChip` spans.
6. **Questions Section (`styles.questionsSection`)** — Optional section (`data-testid="camera-questions"`) rendering `questionsLabel` and `questionChip` pills.

## Multi-Axis Word Pool Architecture

`getCombinedFragmentPool()` in `@nott2/game-engine` merges four thematic axes into deduplicated 55+ word pools for the Active Player (`activePlayerWords`) and the Camera Crew (`cameraCrewWords`), plus Act-specific `cameraQuestions`:

| Axis | Active Player Words (Abstract + Visceral Nouns/Verbs) | Camera Crew Words (Sensory & Atmospheric) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Universal / Playset** | *Generic Slasher*: `MEAT`, `WOUND`, `ELECTRONICS`, `CLANKING`, `THRESHOLD`, `DEADBOLT`<br/>*Summer Camp*: `BOATHOUSE`, `GENERATOR`, `CANOE`, `LANTERN`, `MACHETE` | *Generic Slasher*: `DRIPPING`, `COPPER`, `FLICKER`, `ROT`, `OZONE`, `MILDEW`<br/>*Summer Camp*: `PINE`, `KEROSENE`, `MOSQUITO`, `MUDDY`, `BLACKWATER` |
| **2. Suit** | *Spades*: `LEVERAGE`, `CROWBAR`, `FRACTURE`<br/>*Hearts*: `PARANOIA`, `BETRAYAL`, `PULSE`<br/>*Clubs*: `CIPHER`, `AUTOPSY`, `WIRING`<br/>*Diamonds*: `TIGHTROPE`, `RICOCHET`, `SHARD` | *Spades*: `SWEAT`, `IRON`, `GRINDING`<br/>*Hearts*: `SHIVER`, `WHISPER`, `HEARTBEAT`<br/>*Clubs*: `STATIC`, `FORMALDEHYDE`, `CLICKING`<br/>*Diamonds*: `CREAKING`, `VERTIGO`, `SLICK` |
| **3. Act (1–3)** | *Act 1*: `OMEN`, `CURIOSITY`, `LOCKED`<br/>*Act 2*: `HUNTED`, `TOURNIQUET`, `CORNERED`<br/>*Act 3*: `FINALITY`, `CARNAGE`, `EXECUTION` | *Act 1*: `UNEASY`, `DISTANT`, `DRAFTY`<br/>*Act 2*: `ARTERIAL`, `POUNDING`, `CORNERED`<br/>*Act 3*: `BLINDING`, `CHOKING`, `DEAFENING` |

## Accessibility

- **Disclosure semantics:** The trigger button sets `aria-expanded={false}`, and the open tray renders with `role="region"` and `aria-label={triggerLabel}`.
- **Read-only clarity:** Words and camera questions are rendered in non-interactive `<span>` elements (`userSelect: 'text'`) so assistive technologies do not falsely announce them as buttons or form inputs.
- **Explicit action labels:** The shuffle and close buttons provide clear accessible names (`aria-label="Shuffle nine new spark words"`, `aria-label="Close script ideas"`) and `:focus-visible` outlines.
